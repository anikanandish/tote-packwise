02

The width search

The algorithm tries 200 row widths. For each width it lays the items out in rows and scores the box: volume plus a small penalty on the longest side. The lowest score wins.

03

Placement, step by step

The winning layout, in the order the algorithm places items: longest side first, left to right along a row, a new row when the next item would run past the width. Items stay upright and only turn 90° on the floor.
